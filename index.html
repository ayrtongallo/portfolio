About Me

I'm a Software Engineer with over 3 years of industry experience, bridging enterprise systems development with computational data analysis. My journey into engineering started at just 11 years old, when I taught myself HTML and CSS to build my own websites. That quickly grew into real programming: writing Java to build custom Minecraft mods, and scripting in Pawn to create plugins for Counter-Strike / Half-Life AMX Mod X servers. That early obsession with logic and mechanics is what eventually led me to my first role in the industry, at Globant, working as a Game Quality Analyst for our client, Electronic Arts.

Right now I'm finishing my Technical Degree in Software Development (an Associate Degree), building high-performance backend layers, data persistence layers, and cloud API integrations with .NET (C#). This program has sharpened my focus on object-oriented architecture and advanced database modeling. Alongside it, I'm completing a Postgraduate-level Diploma in Data Science, AI, and its Applications in Economics & Business, which combines statistical predictive modeling with econometrics and machine learning, training me to turn raw data into actionable insight and build analytical pipelines for market and financial analysis.

After graduating, I plan to move into a Bachelor's Degree in Information Technology, where I'll steer my electives toward AI, Machine Learning, Big Data & Analytics, Cloud Computing, and Fintech, continuing to build on my software and systems foundation, before advancing into a Master's degree to keep specializing further.
